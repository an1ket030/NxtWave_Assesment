## The Working Asset

A fully functional **registration + referral growth system** built to acquire 500 final-year engineering students for NxtWave's free AI workshop.

### What it does

- **Landing page** — Output-first messaging designed to convert a final-year student who's seen a hundred generic webinars
- **Registration flow** — 5-field form, under 2 minutes, zero friction
- **Referral system** — Auto-generates a unique referral link per registrant. Pre-written one-tap WhatsApp/LinkedIn/Telegram share
- **Milestone tracker** — 3 career-relevant unlocks (priority seat → AI project evaluation → mentor resume review)
- **Campaign dashboard** — Live registration progress toward 500, channel breakdown, K-factor, cost per registration, top ambassador leaderboard, budget tracker
- **A/B experiment tracker** — 3 live experiments (headline, CTA, referral incentive) that auto-resolve during simulation
- **Simulation mode** — Click "Simulate Campaign Growth" to watch 7-day campaign data fill the dashboard in real time

### How to run

Just open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no build step, no framework.

## Growth Strategy Summary

**Value Proposition:** `Ship your first AI project. In 60 minutes. For free.`

**5 channels:**
1. WhatsApp Campus Ambassadors — 20 colleges, peer trust, 80-90% open rate
2. Peer Referral Loop — every registrant becomes a distribution node
3. LinkedIn + College Club Outreach — 30 targeted DMs to tech club leads
4. Instagram Micro-creator (1 reel, Rs.400) — placement/campus niche
5. Hackathon + Developer Communities — DevFolio, Unstop, Telegram groups
